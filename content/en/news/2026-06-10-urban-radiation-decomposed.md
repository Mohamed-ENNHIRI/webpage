The work of Guillaume Le Gall is out in the International Journal of Thermal Sciences. The subject is higher-order orthogonal decomposition of shortwave radiation in urban settings.

Three-dimensional urban geometry makes incident solar radiation vary across many spatial and temporal scales at once. The paper shows a small number of modes replacing a full simulation of the field.
