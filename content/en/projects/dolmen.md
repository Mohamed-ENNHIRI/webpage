Planning what remains to be installed means knowing what is already there. DOLMEN extends the rooftop photovoltaic detection pipeline developed in the laboratory: aerial imagery, convolutional neural networks, validation against distribution grid operator records, and open dissemination of the results.

The project funds the doctoral work of Mohamed Ennhiri, and builds on the datasets and tools published openly — see the Tools & data page.
